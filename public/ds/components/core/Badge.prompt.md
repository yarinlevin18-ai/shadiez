Small label for status, colorway names, counts, or an eyebrow-style flag.

```jsx
<Badge tone="wood" uppercase>The spectrum</Badge>
<Badge tone="amber" pill>New</Badge>
<Badge tone="outline">Folds flat</Badge>
```

Tones: `sand` (default), `wood`, `navy`, `amber`, `ink`, `outline`. `pill` rounds it; `uppercase` makes it a tracked eyebrow chip.
