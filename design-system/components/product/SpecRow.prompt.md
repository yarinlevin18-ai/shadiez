The quiet inline spec list, with the brand diamond accent between items.

```jsx
<SpecRow items={["Solid walnut", "Cream canvas", "Folds flat"]} />
<SpecRow items={["Blocks the sun", "Adjustable angle"]} separator="rule" />
```

Use under a heading to list materials or selling points. `separator`: `diamond` (amber, default) or `rule` (hairline).
