## ★ Top matches (steal the most from these)

### 1. RIGAS — "exclusive"  → HERO (the #1 match)
- Find: Awwwards → search "furniture" (studio: PROWEB).
- Why: a SINGLE product (a lounge chair) shot outdoors in muted natural light,
  with oversized type ("exclusive") overlapping the image. This is structurally
  our hero: one product outdoors + a huge confident line.
- Steal: product-as-the-whole-stage, oversized "Something New Under The Sun"
  type overlapping the shade, muted natural-light photography, "X years / craft"
  trust line.

### 2. AVO — "Beach"  → OVERALL TONE + TYPE
- Find: Awwwards → search "beach" (studio: Lime Creative).
- Why: editorial, sandy-neutral palette, refined serif wordmark, soft
  photography, generous whitespace. This is exactly SHADIEZ's premium-minimal
  feel and our cream/sand/linen palette.
- Steal: sand/cream neutral system, editorial serif headline treatment,
  whitespace rhythm, calm pacing.

### 3. Hop Farm Beach  → HERO MOOD + MATERIAL STORY
- Find: Awwwards → search "beach" (designer: Cole Roberts).
- Why: warm wood architecture nestled in nature, serif logo, earthy premium —
  sells natural materials and warmth (our walnut frame + canvas).
- Steal: warm wood + nature pairing, premium-natural-material mood, serif logo
  energy for the SHADIEZ wordmark context.
