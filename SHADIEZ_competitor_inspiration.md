# SHADIEZ — Competitor design inspiration

Scan of the premium beach-shade / beach-lifestyle space. Focus: what their sites do well and what SHADIEZ should steal, mapped to our sections.

## The landscape
| Brand | Lane | Visual signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Business & Pleasure Co.** | Retro-luxe beach | Warm film photography, terracotta/cream palette, fringed umbrellas, editorial/magazine feel — **closest visual cousin to SHADIEZ** |
| **Basil Bangs** | Premium designer shade | Rotating editorial hero, named "Collections/Projects", color-swatch product cards, press logos, 3D visualiser |
| **Shibumi Shade** | Direct competitor (beach shade) | Video-first "Setup's a Breeze" hero, named color swatches, huge review counts, UGC testimonials |
| **Sunday Supply Co.** | Vintage coastal | Muted soft palette, minimal, timber + canvas |
| **Neso / Sun Ninja** | Sporty/tech beach tents | Cleaner, bluer, spec-driven, demo videos |

## What to steal — mapped to SHADIEZ sections

### Hero → go video-first (from Shibumi)
Shibumi leads with **"Setup's a Breeze"** and a row of product **videos** (setup, wind, in-use), not a static photo. SHADIEZ's strongest story is *sets up in seconds + your own shade* — show it moving. Keep the confident "Something New Under the Sun" headline, but make the shade a short looping clip (the AE clips we're building are perfect here).

### Colorways → named swatches + live preview (from Shibumi)
Shibumi's color story **"Dream in Color"** uses named swatch chips — *Sunset, Ocean, Coral, Sea Glass, Hibiscus* — each a little SVG swatch that updates the product. SHADIEZ "Pick Your Color" should do exactly this: clickable named swatches (Cream, Coral, Navy, Burgundy…) that swap the shade live. **Highest-value steal.**

### Add a Social-Proof strip (from Shibumi + Basil)
Both lean hard on it: Shibumi shows **"4,294 reviews ★4.9"** and rotating customer quotes with photos; Basil shows star ratings on every card. SHADIEZ has none yet — add a reviews count + 3 short testimonials. Big trust lift on a new brand.

### Add an "As featured in" press row (from Basil Bangs)
Basil runs a **Vogue Living / Monocle / Belle** logo strip. Even modest press/retailer logos read as premium. Add a thin logo band under the hero.

### Editorial "Collection" blocks (from Basil Bangs)
Basil frames drops as **named collections** ("The Italian Collection", "Dolomiti") with cinematic lifestyle blocks ("Latest Project — relaxing never looked so good"). SHADIEZ can frame colorways/lifestyle as a named collection with one big editorial photo block instead of a plain grid.

### Trust badges row (both)
"Free shipping & returns · Limited Lifetime Warranty · Sets up in seconds" — a small icon row near the CTA. Cheap, effective.

### "See it in your space" (from Basil — aspirational)
Basil has a **3D product visualiser** ("Find Your Shade — configure and see it in your space"). SHADIEZ already has a 3D model planned — a lightweight configurator (rotate + swap colorway) would out-class everyone.

### Craft/material storytelling (B&P Co, Sunday Supply)
The premium players sell the **materials**: hardwood poles, marine fixtures, canvas weave, the patented hinge. SHADIEZ's Details hotspots (oak frame, adjustable notch, canvas) are exactly on-trend — keep leaning in.

## Net: SHADIEZ's edge
The category splits into **techy/sporty** (Shibumi, Neso, Sun Ninja) and **retro-luxe/editorial** (B&P Co, Basil, Sunday Supply). SHADIEZ's warm walnut-and-cream, "Bright Coast" look sits firmly in the **premium/editorial** camp — lean further that way (film-grain warmth, big type, named collections) while stealing the **conversion mechanics** the techy brands nail: video hero, named color swatches, loud review proof, trust badges.
